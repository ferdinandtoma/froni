# Launch runbook v2

3 Aug 2026. Supersedes Froni_Launch_Runbook_Draft.md (14 Jul) where they conflict; the draft stands as history. Aligned to SourceOfTruth.md as of the 3 Aug revision. Open values are marked.

## 1. The window

- Cap 200, literal, published. The window closes at the 200th order or 24 hours from open, whichever comes first (ruled 3 Aug).
- Calendar holds Sunday 22 Nov 2026, 18:00 CET list open, 20:00 CET public, with the go or push decision at mid-September, tied to the first sample hoodie. Past mid-September, late November dies and January beats December.
- If day one closes under 200: handling parked until it fires, unresolved by choice (ruled 3 Aug). The 14 Jul threshold tiers (100 plus, 50 to 99, under 50) are superseded by that ruling and await his re-confirmation only if the case fires.
- Overflow past 200 rolls to Edition Two's early door, never fulfilled from Edition One.
- Mechanics per Froni_Edition_One_Window_Scope_03Aug2026.md; five decisions pending.

## 2. Quality control

- 100 percent in-hand inspection against the golden sample.
- A failed face never ships. Remakes come from spare-cut panels inside the factory's overcut; never sold, never packaged.
- Past the remake window the customer chooses wait or refund. Recommended offer point: proactively, the moment the house knows the window will be exceeded. Window value 30 days past the communicated ship week, recommended, unconfirmed.

## 3. Certificate numbering at the packing table

- Numbers run 001 through 200, assigned sequentially as garments are packed, written in ink on the card (locked 27 Jul; supersedes the 14 Jul shuffled deal).
- No blank spares exist (26 Jul). A damaged card is a single-card reprint; replacement handling follows the never-remade rule.
- Each number is logged against its order in Shopify as the box seals. A record for the house, not a promise to anyone.
- Lost-certificate grace: granted once per buyer, tagged in store records. Standing follows the buyer, not the garment; resale does not carry the door.

## 4. Packing days

- Sequence: bone tissue interleaf, hoodie folded hood up, icon face down against the bone, into sleeve, sleeve into box, care card beneath certificate, certificate face up and square, silica hidden under tissue for transatlantic parcels, magnetic lid, double-wall kraft shipper. No candle (26 Jul).
- The sequence is photographed once on the golden sample; the photo is the SOP, taped to the table.
- Sort into four size stacks, process in size waves, one glance at the sewn label before the sleeve closes.
- Numbers dealt sequentially and logged in the same motion as sealing.
- Two people, two to three focused days. Staging needs about 2 cubic metres for two weeks; name the room before packing month.

## 5. Withdrawal and returns

- The 14 day EU withdrawal right applies; made to order does not exempt. The size chart is the practical defense.
- Outside the EU: exchange first, customer pays return shipping.
