# Froni — First videos (draft list)

Drafted July 14, 2026. Twenty concepts, sequenced by what physically exists to film. All faceless, craft-led. Captions here are drafts, not copy — every caption gets the brass plate test before it ships. Standing rules that govern all of it: explain the making, never the meaning; captions carry nouns and dates; no countdowns, no scarcity theater; the comment section does the explaining for free; repeat the subject, vary the frame. Two objects never appear on camera before delivery: the candle and the certificate back.

## Shootable now (July–August) — the icon and the paper

1. The eyes. Slow push-in on the reference scan, ending on the divergent gaze. Caption draft: "Christ Pantocrator. Sinai. Sixth century."
2. The two faces. The mirror composites built on screen — split, flip, side by side. Caption draft: "One face, doubled from each side. The test the embroidery must pass." The comments will explain the theology unprompted; the house never does.
3. The book. Push-in on the jeweled Gospel in the icon's arm. No caption beyond the nouns.
4. The blessing hand. Same treatment.
5. The oldest icon. One line of fact over the full panel: "The oldest surviving icon of Christ." Claim approved in boilerplate July 14; approved here.
6. The render reveal. Black frame, raking light sweeps the back render at scale. First look at what is being made.
7. Why this exists. The thesis, once, sparingly — text on screen, no face, no argument chains. Draft withheld; this one is written together.

## When the trial sew-outs return (roughly late August)

8. First thread. The trial parcel opened, the sewn face at 10 cm, macro.
9. The mirror test, performed. The sewn face photographed, printed twice, cut and mirrored by hand on camera. The strongest single concept on this list.
10. Raking light. The same sew-out under a moving lamp; the texture is the reveal.
11. The gold. Madeira Frosted Matt cones, the marigold family lined up. Caption draft: "Madeira thread, 40 weight."
12. The standard. If a trial fails on scan lines, show what machine-made looks like against hand-directed — no vendor named, ever.

## When strike-offs land (September–October)

13. The full back, first time. 46 cm in raking light.
14. The tape measure. One shot: the icon against the number. Caption draft: "46 centimeters."
15. The wash. The torture swatch, 25 to 30 cycles, before and after, time-lapse. Caption carries the ISO numbers, nothing else.
16. The corridor. The front join under a loupe, the 1 mm registration check. The house showing its own acceptance test.
17. Patina. Tone-on-tone front, new against washed — the front grows more visible with age. Frame as revelation, factually.

## Golden sample and after (October–November)

18. The packing sequence. The SOP photograph session doubles as the film: tissue, sleeve, box, lid. Certificate face-up only; the back never shown; the candle absent from frame.
19. The price answer. Built when the comment arrives, not before: embroidered sacred imagery at Saks sells at $740 to $1,400; this is an edition of 200, made to order. Plain, once.
20. "Is it printed?" The paste-ready plain answer spoken over macro of the stitching. The question already exists; the video waits for the object.

## Notes

- Concepts 1 through 7 need only the scan, the renders, and a screen. The runway can start this month.
- The six angles from the notes map across: craft carries 8 to 17; meaning-as-shown-zone carries 1 to 4; pure visual carries 6 and 13; the thesis is 7; behind-the-build is 18; cultural hooks (feast days) get planned against the calendar separately, never fused with dates in one line.
- Nothing here announces the window or the price. Capture page and bios carry the date; videos carry the making.
