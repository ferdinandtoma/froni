## 1. What the house never writes

The register is defined by absence. Everything below is banned on every surface that leaves the house: site, emails, captions, certificate, packaging, correspondence. The list is law. One violation voids the piece; rewrite, never patch. The gate in section 6 enforces it.

### Mechanisms

No urgency theater: no countdown timers, no stock counters, no only-a-few-left, no deadlines, no almost-gone. Scarcity appears once, as published fact: the cap, the number, never remade. The cap shrinks, never expands.

No discounts, ever. No codes, no sales, no launch pricing, no reactivation bribes, no bundles.

No social proof: no reviews, no testimonials, no trust badges, no press logos, no counts of buyers or subscribers.

No borrowed authority: no endorsements, no as-seen-in, no approvals.

No curiosity bait: no withheld answers, no secrets, no hooks that promise a reveal. What the house shows, it shows plainly; what it does not show, it does not gesture at.

No pain: the copy never describes the buyer's problems, fears, or standing. It does not know them.

No manufactured belonging: no family, no community, no movement. The email field states access as fact and stops.

### Claims

The house never says quality; it cites numbers.

The house never describes itself. Not luxury, not premium, not exclusive, not grave. No adjectives about its own work anywhere. The register is enacted, never named.

The house never argues. Signage carries nouns and dates. The meaning is never explained; the making is. The price is never justified. Objections are never rebutted in copy; they are met by structure, the framing, the care card, the published cap (section 3).

The house never names who the buyer is not.

The house never performs the buyer's reasoning. His monologue stays his (section 3).

The house never claims the face for one communion.

Date and feast never fuse.

Lead times are stated as plain dates, never excused, never blamed on freight. The customer never pays for logistics twice, in money or in prose.

### The merch register

Banned vocabulary, from the evidence: blessed, faith-based, Christian brand, statement piece, drop, rep, repping the faith, conversation starter, witness and testimony and evangelism framings, making anything cool again, verses used as slogans. Some of these appear in the pools' own praise. Banned regardless; the wearer receives, never emits.

### Form

External artifacts: no em dashes, no all-caps headers, no fragment sentences, no explanatory clauses, no credibility-seeking language, no AI punctuation patterns. Plain over clever. Minimal asks: one where one is needed, none where none is. Correspondence signs Regards / Ferdinand Toma / house@froni.co.

No Linktree.

## 2. How the house creates pull

Direct response manufactures pull with urgency, secrets, and pain. The house replaces all of it with one mechanism: specificity. A true noun, a date, a number, stated flatly, pulls the right man harder than any formula, because specificity is the only claim that survives his inspection (section 3), and because this audience does the theology unprompted. Shown the face and the facts, the comments supply the meaning. The house's job is to be the one voice in the room that does not perform.

1. The object carries; the copy points. Photography is object-forward; copy is caption-length by default. When copy grows, it grows into record, not argument.
2. Numbers are the only permitted intensity. The house's superlatives are measurements: grams, centimetres, millimetres, counts, centuries. If a sentence needs an adjective to matter, replace the adjective with a number or cut the sentence.
3. Adjectives describe matter, never worth. Black, marigold, needle-painted, sixth-century pass. Beautiful, rare, exceptional fail.
4. Provenance is the only story, told as chronology. What happened, where, when. No arc, no drama vocabulary, no journey. Story content ends without an ask; the object closes.
5. The making, never the meaning. The house states what was done and what is observable. The two halves of the face are painted differently: the statement ends there. Greek stays Greek. Never-explain is not undiscoverable; the reader who wants the meaning finds it in one search, and finding it himself is the point.
6. Absence does the talking. Every banned mechanism the reader notices missing is a claim the house did not have to make. Section 1 is therefore also pull doctrine.
7. The reference text is the film narration. "One eye is softer. The other more severe." Observation, flat, complete. Every piece is measured against it.
8. Short declaratives. One idea per sentence. A line that only works when read aloud with tone fails the plate (section 6).

## 3. The wearer

### Who he is

He stands at an intersection nobody serves and nobody has measured. On one side, the devout man who already spends real money on devotional objects and defends it as patronage: the laborer is worthy of his hire, piety is not poverty. On the other, the fabric-literate man who reads gsm the way others read logos and defends the spend as buy once, cry once. The wearer is the man in whom both vocabularies already live. The research found his footprints on both banks: the convert whose first icon purchase preceded his conversion because it was the first picture of Jesus that looked like a man to him; the rosary buyer calling money spent in honoring God an investment, not a waste; the denim poster calling heavyweight fabric armor.

Male, roughly 20 to 45, spending power proven in at least one pool. Beyond that the evidence does not go and this section does not invent. No name, no dossier. He is defined by what he believes and what he polices.

### What he already believes

The copy never argues these. He holds the Sinai face as the true portrait, the supreme representation, the one that looks like a man. He knows the asymmetry or discovers it in the comments within minutes; the audience does the theology unprompted. He believes quality is a moral position: a cheap devotional object insults the object and its subject at once. He believes a made thing carries its making.

### What he polices

His first reflex toward any Christian garment is suspicion. His cringe detector is fully trained: vapid, leveraging religion for sales, making Christianity cool again. One false note files the house with the mockery permanently. His second reflex is the Pharisee worry, conspicuous religiosity as pride, and he polices himself harder than he polices brands. Consequence: the copy's job is not persuasion. It is passing inspection by a man reading for the tell.

### The objections and how the house meets them

1. Profanation. The Orthodox objection: an icon torn from prayer loses its telos. Met by framing, which canon already fixes: the house sells a needle-painted reproduction with provenance. It never claims sacramental function, never frames devotionally. The making is the subject.
2. Washing. Met by the care card stating facts. The house never argues reverence.
3. Commercialization. Met by the literal published cap, no discounts, no urgency, commerce whispering. Scarcity as fact is the one form of scarcity this man does not read as a trick.
4. The Pharisee. Met by the house never framing the garment as testimony. Banned framings: conversation starter, rep the faith, statement piece, evangelism tool. Note that the first two appear in the pools' own praise. Even his compliments contain language the house must refuse. The wearer receives.

### His monologue and the house's job

The justification phrase bank is his internal monologue, not house copy. He must defend two hundred twenty euros to himself, and the defense already exists in his head in two dialects. The house's job is to supply the nouns the monologue needs and nothing more: 420 to 450 grams, needle-painted, the stitch count, the days, Portugal, 200, the number on his garment. He assembles buy once cry once and the laborer is worthy of his hire on his own. If the copy ever says his lines for him, it collapses into the merch it is not. This is the existing law, the house never says quality, it cites numbers, extended one step: the house never performs his reasoning either.

### Language

May appear in copy because he trusts it: Pantocrator, Sinai, sixth century, encaustic, needle-painted, gsm, French terry, made in Portugal, edition, numbered, the observable facts of the face stated flatly.

Never in copy: his own monologue (buy once cry once, armor, BIFL, heirloom talk), the merch register (blessed, faith-based, Christian brand, statement piece, drop), and every word from the mockery bank's target list.

### Where he talks

r/Catholicism, r/OrthodoxChristianity, r/rawdenim, r/goodyearwelt, the comment sections under Sinai Pantocrator videos. No platform proves the intersection; the funnel discovers him through first-party data, per canon. Orthodox men will arrive because the image predates the schism. The copy never claims the face for one communion; the making-forward register is the shared ground.

### Who he is not

The hypebeast who bought the Supreme Jesus hoodie to flip it. The youth-group merch buyer. The edgelord buying friction. All three have money. The house does not write for them, and the register that wins the wearer repels them without effort.

## 5. Sequence architecture

Consent: German double opt-in per § 7 UWG. The confirmation email carries zero marketing. The welcome clock starts at the confirmation click, not the form.

Welcome: five emails. Day 0, Froni. Day 2, The face. Day 5, The making. Day 8, The edition; price appears here and nowhere earlier. Day 12, What follows. Days adjustable; the order is not: house, object, making, mechanics and price, cadence.

After welcome: no calendar. The list is event-driven; a send requires a true making event: sew-out approved, cloth knitted, garments entering production, edition opened, edition allocated, garments shipping. Silence between events is the register holding. The three-to-one value ratio of direct response is replaced by this rule entirely.

Launch window: one opening send, one allocated send, and between them only true events. Nothing that reads as a countdown.

Segments: buyers via Placed-Order; they continue to receive the record plus transactional mail. Non-buyers after allocation receive the record and Edition Two first word, per the overflow canon. No behavioral micro-segmentation.

Hygiene, per the 27 Jul 2026 ruling: nobody is sunset during Edition One. A suppression candidate after Edition One needs at least 12 months on the list, at least five post-welcome emails, no verified click, no order and no other deliberate response. One plain retention notice (4.9); no response within 30 days leads to marketing suppression, not deletion; no second notice. Unsubscribes, spam complaints and hard bounces are suppressed immediately. Protects deliverability. No discount, no plea.

Measurement: first-party UTM only for funnel truth; platform engagement metrics only for hygiene. External benchmarks are not calibration; re-base within two weeks of live posting.

Infrastructure, for the record: Klaviyo server-side relay via Cloudflare Pages Function, no script on the page; PostHog EU, cookieless server hash, in or out unruled. Copy never mentions any of it.

## 6. The gate

Every piece passes every check or is rewritten whole. Never patch a failing piece.

1. Read aloud once. Any sentence that needs performance (tone, irony, emphasis) to work fails.
2. The plate. The bell test's copy form: every line must hold as if engraved on the brass plate at the door. Nouns, dates, numbers, standing alone, no voice to help them. A line that embarrasses on brass fails on screen.
3. The section 1 sweep, mechanical: urgency, discount, social proof, authority, curiosity bait, pain, belonging, self-description, argument, price justification, the buyer's monologue, communion claims, date-feast fusion, merch vocabulary; and in external artifacts, em dashes, all-caps headers, fragment sentences, explanatory clauses. Any hit fails the piece.
4. The wearer mirror. Read once as the man in section 3, reading for the tell. If any line could be read as merch, or performs his reasoning for him, fail.
5. Numbers. Every figure traces to the tech pack or the record. Bracketed figures never publish. No invented number survives, even as placeholder, in anything external.
6. One ask maximum; zero where none is needed.
7. Last, read the piece against the reference text (2.7). If the register sits below it, rewrite.

This section is the specification for the copy linter. Checks 3 and 5 and the form rules are mechanically testable and should be implemented as such.

## 7. Translations

The direct-response move beside the house's answer. For calibration, not production.

Urgency: "Only 12 left, don't miss out" becomes: 200, numbered, never remade.

Discount reactivation: "We miss you, here's 15% off" becomes: This address has not answered the house's mail in twelve months. If nothing follows within 30 days, no further mail is sent.

Social proof: "Loved by 10,000 customers" becomes: This garment is No. 143 of 200.

Curiosity bait: "The secret behind this design will shock you" becomes: The two halves of the face are painted differently.

Pain: "Tired of cheap hoodies that fall apart?" becomes: [grams] grams per square metre.

Authority: "As seen in GQ" becomes: The face was painted at Sinai in the sixth century.

Belonging: "Join the family" becomes: The address receives the record of the making and first notice when the edition opens.

Hype close: "Grab yours now before it's gone!" becomes: Edition One is open.
