# Packaging brief v2

3 Aug 2026. Supersedes Froni_Packaging_Supplier_Brief.pdf, which is stale (150 and 160 unit counts, the candle, a 200,000 stitch placeholder). Requirements only. Complete means each line can be quoted as a number.

## Quantities

- 200 shipped units, one edition, one production run.
- Order quantities allow overage for QC and one packing rehearsal: 210 boxes, 210 sleeves, 420 tissue sheets, 200 certificate cards numbered 001 to 200 plus none blank, 210 care cards, 210 shippers.

## Components

1. Box: rigid, magnetic lid. Interior sized to a folded heavyweight hoodie (420 to 450 gsm, sizes S to XL). Target outer dimensions within 60 x 30 x 15 cm, proposed, unconfirmed.
2. Sleeve: garment sleeve inside the box.
3. Tissue: bone colour, two sheets per unit.
4. Certificate card: carries a printed number, 001 to 200, position for ink. Stock accepts archival pigment ink; pen test on sample stock required.
5. Care card: same stock family as certificate.
6. Shipper: double-wall kraft, sized to the box.
7. Silica: food-safe sachet, hidden under tissue, transatlantic parcels only.

## Constraints

- Packed parcel targets under 2 kg total (locked 26 Jul).
- Packaging cost ceiling 15 euros per order, all components in (locked 28 Jul).
- Nothing metallic. No printed marketing anywhere. The box carries the wordmark at most; surfaces otherwise blank.
- Samples of box, sleeve, card stock and shipper before order; one full packed rehearsal on the golden sample.

## Asked of the supplier

Unit prices at 210, lead time from artwork approval, sample lead time and cost, stock weights and colour references for tissue and card.
