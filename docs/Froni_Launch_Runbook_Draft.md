# Froni — Launch Runbook (draft)

Compiled July 14, 2026. This restates Ferdinand's existing decisions in one operational document; anything not his decision is marked open or drafted. Plain words throughout. Updated same day: edition set at 200.

## 1. Sell-through thresholds (decided)

- 100 or more orders: produce as planned.
- 50 to 99: small-batch fallback tier; unit cost rises; accepted.
- Under 50: full refunds, publish nothing defensive, keep the list, rework.
- Sellout inside the window: the cap stands at 200, no extension ever; overflow demand rolls to Edition Two's early door.

## 2. Per-garment quality control (decided)

- 100% in-hand inspection of every unit against the golden sample.
- A failed face never ships. Remakes come from spare-cut panels; the factory's ±5% overcut maps onto this as remake and QC stock, never sold, never packaged.
- Past the remake window the customer chooses: wait or refund. Recommended value, awaiting yes: 30 days past the communicated ship week, offered proactively the moment the house knows the window will be exceeded.

## 3. Certificate numbering at the packing table (decided this week)

- Printer delivers 200 cards numbered 1 to 200 plus 10 with the number position blank.
- The numbered stack is shuffled once, then dealt onto boxes as they seal. Random at the table; never tied to purchase order; never requested by a buyer.
- Each number is logged against its order in Shopify as the box seals — a record for the house, not a promise to anyone.
- A damaged numbered card is replaced by a blank spare completed by hand in archival pigment ink; test the pen on sample stock when samples arrive. Beyond one or two losses, a single-card reprint from the printer.

## 4. Lost-certificate grace (decided)

- Granted once per buyer, verified against the buyer cohort, tagged in store records.
- House line on granting: "Granted, this once. The certificate is the record of your standing; the next door asks for it again."
- Standing follows the buyer, not the garment. Resale does not carry the door.

## 5. Packing days (decided, October fills the blanks)

- Sequence: bone tissue interleaf → hoodie folded hood-up, icon face-down against the bone → into sleeve → sleeve into box → care card beneath certificate → certificate face-up, square → silica hidden under tissue for transatlantic parcels → magnetic lid → double-wall kraft shipper.
- The candle packs beneath the tissue with the rest; it is never shown in content before delivery.
- The sequence is photographed once on the golden sample in October; the photo is the SOP, taped to the table.
- Size is the one surviving matching risk: sort into four size stacks, process in size waves, one glance at the sewn label before the sleeve closes.
- Certificate numbers are dealt and logged in the same motion (section 3).
- Two people, two to three focused days, late January, with roughly three days of slack before Candlemas. Staging needs about 2 m³ for two weeks; name the room before January.

## 6. Withdrawal and returns (statutory, noted)

- The 14-day EU withdrawal right applies; made-to-order does not exempt. The merciless size chart is the practical defense.
- Outside the EU: exchange first, customer pays return shipping.

## 7. Open items this runbook waits on

- Remake window value in section 2: 30 days recommended, not yet confirmed.
- Payment processor rolling reserves and charge timing — resolve before October.
- Fabric pre-booking decision — lands with factory quotes.
- Exact ship-week wording for the preorder page (a week, not a range).
- Candle sourcing: church-grade beeswax taper, monastery supplier preferred, sized to the box interior once packaging samples exist.
