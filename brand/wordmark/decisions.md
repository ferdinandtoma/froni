# FRONI wordmark decisions

Append-only log. Each entry is one verdict on one parameter snapshot. Entries
are never rewritten or reordered.

## HARD BAN (never facilitated by any control)

- NO cross hidden in any letterform. NO cruciform counters or gaps. The lab
  offers no control that splits a counter or crosses a stroke, and no verdict
  may approve a mark that reads as one.

## Design canon

- The mark is the word FRONI alone. Wordmark-only house: no symbol, no
  tagline, no lowercase. Capitals only.
- Register: late-antique epigraphic capitals. Wide, stable letterforms, flared
  carved terminals, generous inscription spacing. DNA by eye only: Albertus,
  Optima, Friz Quadrata, Alverata. Never download or embed the proprietary
  references.
- Dead lanes, never rendered: hairline didones, blackletter, raw Trajan clones.
- One weight. No italics anywhere.
- One gesture maximum, mutually exclusive: none | raised small O | lozenge O.
- Palette: tenebrae #131114, porphyry #4E2A3A, bone #EDE6D6, matte gold
  #C9A227. Gold is ink only, never a ground.
