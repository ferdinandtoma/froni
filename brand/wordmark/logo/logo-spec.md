# FRONI wordmark - locked spec

Approved 2026-07-08.

## The mark
- Word: **FRONI**, capitals only, one weight, no lowercase.
- Typeface: **Gilda Display** by **Eduardo Tunni** (a high-contrast old-style
  display serif), SIL Open Font License. Attribution per the font's own OFL:
  "Copyright 2012 The Gilda Display Project Authors
  (github.com/etunni/gilda-display)"; Google Fonts credits Eduardo Tunni.
- Tracking: **+0.04 em** (letter-spacing 4% of cap size).
- Case: all caps. Never set lowercase or italic.

## Colour
- Primary: **bone #EDE6D6 on tenebrae #131114** (the approved lockup).
- Reversed: tenebrae #131114 on bone #EDE6D6.
- Accent: **gold #C9A227** on tenebrae, for the theonym-adjacent / ceremonial use only.
- Gold is ink only, never a ground.

## Usage rules, physical media
- Care label: printed tape, not woven. Wordmark minimum 20 mm wide on the label.
- The wax seal carries the enthroned-cross mark, never the wordmark.
- The wordmark is never blind-embossed.
- Any stitched or woven instance of the name uses the carved cut (see carved/ below), never the Gilda cut.
- Porphyry #4E2A3A may serve as ground for bone or gold ink. Gold remains ink only, never a ground.

## Small mark (F monogram)
- The Gilda "F" monogram is the official small mark of the house, scoped to favicon, app icons, and social avatars only. Never on the garment, never as a primary mark on packaging or print.

## Carved cut (physical media)
- carved/ is the authoritative carved cut of the wordmark (B-001, the glyphic Albertus cut) for stitched, woven, and embossed applications; its thinnest stroke measures ~0.91 mm at 8 mm cap, clearing the satin-stitch floor.

## Files (all outlined paths, no font dependency, no live text)
- FRONI-wordmark.svg - master, currentColor, transparent (recolour per use).
- FRONI-wordmark-{bone,tenebrae,gold}.svg - single-colour, transparent.
- FRONI-wordmark-bone-on-tenebrae.svg - primary lockup with ground.
- PNG rasters at 2048 / 1024 / 512 / 128 px width per colourway.

## Clear space & minimum size
- Clear space: at least the height of the cap "F" on all sides (baked into the SVG viewBox as ~9% padding).
- Minimum legible size (screen/print): ~90 px / 24 mm wide before the hairline serifs start to break.

## Embroidery caveat (important for the garment)
Gilda Display is a HIGH-CONTRAST serif. At an 8 mm cap the thin serifs measure
~0.19 mm - below the ~0.8 mm satin-stitch floor. Do NOT embroider this cut small.
For stitched applications use a heavier, lower-contrast cut of the same wordmark
(the lab's Track B at contrast ~0.4 clears the floor), or run it large enough
(chest/back scale) that the hairlines hold. Reserve the fine Gilda cut for print,
web, and woven labels.
